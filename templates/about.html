About World Data


World Data is a project envisioned to create a data and science-focused repository, with a special emphasis on astronomy and space. Its objective is to develop tools and infrastructure for collecting, processing, and utilizing scientific data, transforming it into actionable information. The project’s development trajectory begins with ground-based and observatory initiatives and has the potential to expand into satellite systems and space-based projects in the future.



Goals & Projects


Goals

World Data aims to develop scientific and technological projects centered around data, astronomy, and space. Our long-term goal is to create useful scientific infrastructure, develop new technologies, and make scientific data more accessible and practical.


Projects

Our projects are planned as a long-term path that begins on Earth and gradually expands toward space.


Phase 1 — Earth:

Development of astronomical infrastructure, including observatories, telescopes, and a network for astronomical observation and data collection, with an initial focus on Iran.


Phase 2 — Earth Orbit:

Development of satellite-based projects, including practical Earth-observation satellites for applications such as mapping, agriculture, and environmental monitoring, as well as astronomy satellites designed for space-based observation and research.
