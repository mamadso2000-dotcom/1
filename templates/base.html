World Data

World Data is a project envisioned to create a data and science-focused repository with a special emphasis on astronomy and space. Its objective is to develop tools and infrastructure for collecting, processing, and utilizing scientific data, transforming it into actionable information. The project’s development trajectory begins with ground-based and observatory initiatives and has the potential to expand into satellite systems and space-based projects in the future.
